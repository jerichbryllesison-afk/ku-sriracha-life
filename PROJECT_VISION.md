# Project Vision — KU Sriracha Life

An online (one server, max 100 players) third-person student-life simulation set on the real map of
**Kasetsart University, Sriracha Campus** and surrounding Sriracha (Sukhumvit Road to the Ao Udom coast).

**Player fantasy:** be a KU Sriracha student — register, attend classes, keep needs up, work part-time jobs,
earn and spend baht, buy clothes and vehicles, explore a recognisable campus, meet other students online.

**Tone:** warm, everyday Thai campus life; believable rather than fantastical. GTA-like freedom of movement,
without violence.

**Pillars**
1. *Recognisable place* — real road network and building footprints; real campus names (Thai + English).
2. *Daily rhythm* — 48-minute game day, classes 08:00–17:00, needs, allowance, day/night and weather.
3. *Shared campus* — one persistent world, chat, money transfers, visible other players.
4. *Progression* — levels, skills, GPA, main story (15 quests), dailies, side tasks, collectibles.

## Living-world directive (2026-10-28)
Open-world multiplayer university life simulation on the real KU Sriracha map: every player has an identity, daily life,
housing, a vehicle, friends, a phone, work, income, study, relationships, progress and freedom; the world has NPCs with
individual lives, shops and businesses, delivery / ride services, GPS, sports, an online community, 3-D sound, animals and
events that react to players. Systems must connect (no isolated features), the world must feel alive without nagging
(activity director with cooldowns, player can always just explore), and the current graphics are the fixed visual baseline.
Delivered as tested milestones (see `docs/GAMEPLAY_SYSTEMS.md`).
